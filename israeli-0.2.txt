My Grand Father Keith Ezzy Abrams was brought up in a house called Merrindale, Kurrajong, Hawkesbury, NSW.  His two Uncles, Uncle Hubert and Edwin were there and a cousin called Louise.  They brought up Keith Ezzy Abrams like royalty, he was in a bubble.  Catherine my Great Great  Grand Mother brought up Keith on the English dictionary and I believe and Victorian manners.  He rode a horse and went to the First World War.  He survived as he was in Artillery and then Messaging.  He came back and joined the bank but i think he found this difficult.  He got married when he came back and after the bank he was given an orchard by his Uncle Herbert in Kurrajong NSW.  He wasted all his surplus money given to him by Catherine I believe. When in Kurrajong he built an American house which would have been beautiful.  Unfortunately there was a 10 year drought and the a bush fire about 1943 put him out of business,  I think he was in trouble anyway as he was not really Agricultural. Keith did not have much of a life after this, I believe. My mother was born 1927, Catherine came to give the baton to my mother but died just before seeing her birth, close to that.  My mother would said this was a Great Aunt but what Great Aunt, I put the picture together, this was Catherine.  Catherine also had delusions of royalty.
